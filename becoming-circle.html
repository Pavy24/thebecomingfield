Small group spaces in nature

The Becoming Circle

An outdoor practice where meditation, inner parts work, somatic work and Reiki come together, held in the quiet of nature.

What it is

A shared field, held in nature

The Becoming Circle is a small group space, a place where a journey inspired by meditation and inner parts work comes together with Reiki.

We gather outdoors, in nature. Being in a natural setting softens the mind and opens what tends to stay closed indoors. The group field allows something unique to emerge, a shared holding that deepens both individual and collective becoming.

You don't need any prior experience. You only need a willingness to slow down and meet what is alive in you, at your own pace.

The shape of the work

Four movements

Connection

We arrive, settle the nervous system through breath and come into contact with the ground, the body, the group and the living space around us.

Awareness

Through slow movement and gentle attention, we let the body show us where it holds, listening rather than analysing, noticing what is here.

Release

We meet the tension we find with care, pendulation, parts dialogue and Reiki, allowing what is ready to soften and move.

Integration

Solo time in nature, optional sharing and a collective closing, so that what opened has space to settle and become yours.

How a session unfolds

A gentle, unhurried flow

Each gathering moves through a natural arc, roughly ninety minutes, with long pauses and no pressure to perform:

  • Settling — breathwork to arrive and calm the nervous system
  • Intention — an energy practice to set what you bring and what you're ready to release
  • Movement & nature contact — slow movement that opens into the natural space around us
  • Pendulation — meeting two or three zones of tension the body reveals
  • Parts work — a guided dialogue with the most alive part, step by step
  • Group Reiki — a few minutes of shared energetic holding
  • Solo integration — quiet time in a spot in nature you choose
  • Sharing & closing — an optional circle, then a collective close

You started under siege. You went through what was there. And you always arrived in the same place, a peaceful, supportive, reconciled emptiness. From that space you can move forward.

Practical details

Where & when

We meet in nature, in a quiet outdoor setting with room to spread out and be held by the space around us.

Group size is kept small, so the field stays intimate and safe. The exact meeting point, timing and what to bring are shared with you after you join, so we can hold the space with care and keep it for those who are truly coming.
