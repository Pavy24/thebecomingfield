The Life Energy Audit

Behind the performance, a guided self-inquiry that shows you where your energy goes and where it is quietly asking to return.

What it is

A quiet look beneath the surface

So much of our energy lives in the upper field, the situations, the deadlines, the relationships, the constant effort of holding it all together. When something unsettles us, we tend to look outward for the cause.

The Life Energy Audit gently turns that around. It rests on a simple premise: what you feel begins in you. Rather than fixing the outside, it helps you notice where your energy leaks, where you brace and where a part of you is waiting to be met.

It is not a diagnosis or a label. It is a mirror, an honest, unhurried picture of your inner landscape right now.

What it explores

Four areas of life

Identity & value

How you see your own worth, whether it rests in you or depends on what you do and how others respond.

Relationship with others

How you meet the people around you, where you give too much, hold back or lose yourself in connection.

Relationship with life

How you meet what life brings, uncertainty, change, the things you cannot control and where it tightens you.

Relationship with self

How you treat yourself underneath it all, the inner tone, the demands, the care or its absence.

How it works

Three simple steps

  • Answer honestly — around fifteen minutes of quiet, sincere questions. There are no right answers, only true ones.
  • Receive your picture — a clear reflection of where your energy flows freely and where it is held, across the four areas.
  • Take the next step, if you wish — with your results in hand, you can explore what wants attention, on your own or together.

The work begins with one short, sincere sentence about what is true right now: the moment attention stops flowing outward and begins to flow home.
